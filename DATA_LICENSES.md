# Data licence

EXIOBASE version: 3.8.2

The official Zenodo record states that EXIOBASE 3.8.2 is provided under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike licence (CC-BY-SA).

Raw EXIOBASE archives are downloaded during builds, stored in `cache/`, and not committed.

> Stadler, K. et al. EXIOBASE 3: Developing a Time Series of Detailed Environmentally Extended Multi-Regional Input-Output Tables. Journal of Industrial Ecology 22, 502–515 (2018); EXIOBASE 3.8.2 dataset, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.5589597.

> Hickel, J., Hanbury Lemos, M. & Barbour, F. Unequal exchange of labour in the world economy. Nature Communications 15, 6298 (2024).
